# Interested Parties

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify the internal and external interested parties that influence CloudCart's Information Security Management System (ISMS). Understanding their needs and expectations helps ensure that the ISMS addresses relevant business, legal, contractual, and regulatory requirements.

---

## Interested Parties and Their Expectations

| Interested Party | Expectations | Information Security Requirement | Why It Matters |
|-----------------|--------------|----------------------------------|----------------|
| Customers | Secure platform, protection of personal and business data | Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability | Customer trust and business reputation |
| Employees | Secure working environment, reliable IT systems | Secure authentication, endpoint protection, awareness training | Enables employees to perform their duties securely |
| Executive Management | Reduced business risk, regulatory compliance, business continuity | Effective governance and risk management | Supports strategic business objectives |
| Information Security Team | Resources, management support, security tools | Continuous monitoring and improvement | Enables implementation and maintenance of the ISMS |
| Regulators | Compliance with applicable laws and regulations | Legal and regulatory compliance | Avoids legal penalties and protects reputation |
| AWS (Cloud Provider) | Shared responsibility for cloud security | Secure cloud configuration and access management | Critical infrastructure supporting CloudCart services |
| Microsoft | Secure Microsoft 365 environment | Identity management and data protection | Supports secure communication and collaboration |
| Payment Gateway Provider | Secure payment processing | Secure integration and vendor management | Protects financial transactions |
| Business Partners | Reliable and secure services | Availability and secure data exchange | Maintains trusted business relationships |
| External Auditors | Evidence of ISMS implementation | Documented policies, controls, and records | Demonstrates ISO 27001 compliance readiness |

---

## Compliance Obligations

Based on the identified interested parties, CloudCart shall consider the following obligations while implementing the ISMS.

- Compliance with applicable privacy regulations (e.g., DPDP Act, GDPR where applicable)
- Protection of customer information
- Fulfilment of contractual security requirements
- Maintenance of service availability
- Secure management of cloud infrastructure
- Protection of intellectual property
- Support for continual improvement of the ISMS

---

## Conclusion

Understanding the needs and expectations of interested parties enables CloudCart to establish an Information Security Management System that aligns with business objectives while satisfying legal, regulatory, contractual, and customer requirements.
